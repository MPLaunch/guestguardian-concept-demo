Demand patterns

Three very different markets

The coast

Glenelg, Henley, Brighton, Semaphore. Heavily seasonal, premium summer weekends, strong minimum-stay leverage over December and January.

City & inner

CBD, North Adelaide, Norwood, Unley. Event-driven peaks plus steady corporate midweek. Rate moves fast and needs watching daily.

Hills & wine

Hahndorf, McLaren Vale, Mount Barker. Longer stays, weekend-weighted, driven by cellar doors and getaway demand rather than events.

Featured area

Glenelg

Adelaide's strongest short-stay market, and the one where getting pricing wrong costs the most. Summer weekends carry the year.

Beachfront apartments, marina frontage and heritage cottages within walking distance of Jetty Road and the tram. Demand concentrates hard into December, January and event weekends, then drops away — which is exactly why a static nightly rate fails here.
