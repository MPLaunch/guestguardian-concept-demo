One honest caveat about "protecting it". In this demo the lock is done in the visitor's browser, which means somebody technical could get around it if they really wanted to. Fees

Questions about cost

Why is management a percentage rather than a flat fee?
Because it aligns us. If we do not lift your revenue, we do not lift our own. A flat fee gets paid whether the calendar is full or empty.
What is not included in the management fee?
Cleaning is charged to the guest as a separate turnover fee, which is industry standard. Consumables, maintenance work and any furnishing spend are billed at cost and appear itemised on your statement.
Are there setup fees?
Photography and listing build are included from Full Service upward. On Essentials they are quoted separately so you are not paying for something you may already have.
Is there a lock-in contract?
No. Rolling agreement, 30 days notice. We would rather earn the renewal every month.
When do I get paid?
Monthly, with a statement that itemises every booking and every expense. No mystery deductions.
