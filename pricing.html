Fees

Questions about cost

Why is management a percentage rather than a flat fee?
Because it aligns us. If we do not lift your revenue, we do not lift our own. A flat fee gets paid whether the calendar is full or empty.
What is not included in the management fee?
Cleaning is charged to the guest as a separate turnover fee. We restock linen, towels and essential consumables, and manage repairs through trusted local trades.
Are there setup fees?
Photography and listing build are included from Full Service upward. On Essentials they are quoted separately so you are not paying for something you may already have.
When do I get paid?
Monthly, with a statement for the period. You also get regular access to your booking information and performance.
