Concept note for Nick — the "make them fill in the form first" bit.

This is now built the way you asked. Nobody sees a number until they hand over their details, so every person curious enough to work out what their property could earn becomes a named enquiry sitting in your inbox rather than an anonymous visitor who quietly leaves.

One honest caveat about "protecting it". In this demo the lock is done in the visitor's browser, which means somebody technical could get around it if they really wanted to. On the real build we would do the sum on the server instead, so the figures are simply not sent to the page until the form is submitted. Same experience for a normal visitor, but the numbers genuinely cannot be lifted. Worth knowing, because it is the difference between looking locked and being locked.

And the data behind it. The figures here are still Adelaide market averages. Pointing this at your own booking and pricing history is exactly what the Hostaway connection below unlocks, and it is what turns this from a nice widget into something you can defend to an owner.
