Local knowledge

Why Glenelg is different

Glenelg concentrates its revenue. December and January, event weekends, cruise ship calls and the run of Mad March overflow from the city do a disproportionate share of the year's takings. Miss those and the annual number never recovers, no matter how well the shoulder season runs.

Position matters more here than almost anywhere in Adelaide. Walking distance to Jetty Road, the jetty and the tram, or frontage onto the Patawalonga marina, separates a strong performer from an average one — and guests price that difference accurately.

  • Walk-to-Jetty-Road and beachfront positioning priced accordingly
  • Minimum-stay strategy tuned to summer and long weekends
  • Event and cruise-call calendar built into pricing
  • Marina, beachfront tower, heritage cottage and townhouse all handled
  • Strata and by-law position checked before listing

Glenelg questions

Straight answers

How much can a Glenelg Airbnb earn?
It depends on bedrooms, position and presentation. Beachfront and marina-front apartments achieve materially higher summer weekend rates than equivalent properties a few streets back. We give you a written appraisal with a realistic nightly rate and occupancy estimate before you commit to anything.
Is Glenelg too seasonal to be worth it?
No, but it has to be managed as a seasonal asset. The peak has to be maximised and the shoulder has to be worked. Owners who set one rate in November and leave it are the ones who conclude it does not stack up.
Do you manage apartments in the towers?
Yes, including buildings with by-laws around short-stay. We check the strata position before listing so there are no surprises.
What about parking and the tram?
Both are genuine booking drivers in Glenelg and both belong in the listing. If your property has secure parking, that alone lifts your rate — it should not be buried in the description.
