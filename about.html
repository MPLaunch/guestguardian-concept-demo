Our approach

Assertive, not passive

Plenty of managers will list your property and wait. We take positions: on what the rate should be tonight, on whether a booking should be accepted, on whether the property is presented well enough to charge what it should.

That is what "assertive" means in our name. It is not aggression toward guests — our ratings show the opposite. It is a refusal to let a property drift because nobody wanted to make a call.

  • Adelaide owned and operated, not a national franchise
  • We manage our own properties on the same system
  • Every owner speaks to a person who knows their property
  • No lock-in — we earn the renewal monthly
  • Pricing published openly on the website

What we stand on

Three commitments

Protect the asset

Screening, inspections and photographed condition checks. The property comes back the way it went out, or we find out immediately and act.

Maximise the return

Daily repricing against real demand, minimum-stay strategy and presentation advice. The revenue number is the job.

Tell you the truth

If short-stay is wrong for your property, we will say so. A bad fit costs us more in reputation than it earns in fees.
