The problem we solve

Rental management shouldn't be a bad experience

Most owners we meet are not short on bookings. They are short on sleep, short on answers, and short on the net figure they were promised.

You are still the one on call

Lockbox codes at midnight, a broken air conditioner on a Sunday, a guest who will not leave. "Managed" should mean your phone stays quiet.

Nobody is actually pricing it

A flat nightly rate set once and forgotten leaves thousands on the table across a year of events, school holidays and shoulder seasons.

Anyone can book it

Without real screening, the wrong booking becomes your problem, your damage claim and your review. Vetting is not optional.

The difference

A holistic management approach

Three disciplines that most managers treat as one. We run them separately, and properly.

Listing management

Professional photography, styling guidance and channel-optimised listings across Airbnb, Stayz and Booking.com — then repriced every day against live demand.

Guest management

Screening before approval, 24/7 communication, hotel-grade turnovers with premium linen, and a photographed condition check after every stay.

Rental management

Maintenance coordination, routine inspections, compliance and insurance oversight, and a live dashboard showing exactly how the asset is performing.

03 / 06

The process

From first call to first booking

Four steps, roughly seven to ten days. You approve the rate and the listing; we do everything else.

01

Appraisal

We look at the property, the position and the comparable set, then put a realistic nightly rate and occupancy estimate in writing. If short-stay is wrong for the property, this is where we say so.

02

Setup

Photography, listing build across Airbnb, Stayz and Booking.com, pricing rules, compliance check and any styling work. Typically seven to ten days from go-ahead.

03

Live

Listings go live. Screening and 24/7 guest cover start from the very first enquiry, so nothing lands on your phone.

04

Optimise

Rate, minimum stays and presentation reviewed continually against live demand. You watch all of it on the owner dashboard.

Common questions

Straight answers

What does short-term rental management cost in Adelaide?
Our management fee is a percentage of the revenue we generate, so we only do well when you do. Full detail, including what is and is not included, is on our pricing page — no phone call required to see a number.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. We work on a rolling agreement with 30 days notice. If we are not adding value, you should be free to leave.
Do you screen guests, or does the platform do it?
We screen every booking ourselves before it is approved. Platform verification is a starting point, not a substitute. Read more on guest screening.
What if my property needs furnishing first?
We can style and furnish from scratch or refresh what is already there. Presentation is the single biggest driver of nightly rate.
Which suburbs do you cover?
Metropolitan Adelaide plus the Hills, McLaren Vale and the coast. See the full list of areas.
