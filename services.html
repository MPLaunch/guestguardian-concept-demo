Questions

About the service

Can I choose only some services?
Yes. Most owners take the full service because the parts compound, but if you already have a cleaner you are happy with, we will work around them.
Do you manage the property if I want to use it myself?
Of course. Block out owner dates whenever you like through the dashboard, and we will work the calendar around you.
How quickly can you get a property live?
Typically seven to ten days from go-ahead: photography, listing build, pricing setup and compliance check. Faster if the property is already furnished and photographed.
What happens if a guest damages something?
We photograph after every stay, so damage is identified immediately and claimed while the evidence is fresh. You are told the same day, not at the end of the month.
